# After the Rain

An interactive 3D listening lounge at dusk. A record turns by the window while a listener sits with a steaming mug.

[Open the room](https://dongwhfdyer.github.io/after-the-rain/), or download `index.html` and open it directly in a browser with WebGL enabled. The file includes the scene, its procedural models and textures, Three.js, and OrbitControls.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, or choose **Play tour** to move between the room, record bar, and window seat. Press **H** to hide or restore the controls.

Three.js and OrbitControls are distributed under the MIT license included in the HTML.
